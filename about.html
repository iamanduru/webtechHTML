ABOUT NGAO

Helping people respond better to suspicious digital activity.

Ngao is a digital scam and phishing reporting portal designed to help users document suspicious messages, recognise common warning signs and keep track of submitted reports.

01

THE PROBLEM

Digital scams can be difficult to recognise.

Suspicious messages can arrive through email, SMS, WhatsApp and social media. They may appear to come from banks, employers, government institutions, companies or even people we know.

Many users may not immediately recognise the warning signs or know what to do after receiving suspicious digital content.

02

OUR APPROACH

One simple place to report, review and learn.

Ngao provides a simple platform where users can submit reports about suspicious digital activity and view information about common scam patterns.

Report suspicious activity

Users can document suspicious messages, links, emails or online interactions.

Track submitted reports

Users can view reports they have submitted and see their current review status.

Learn warning signs

Ngao provides basic guidance to help users recognise common characteristics of scams and phishing attempts.

MISSION

Make digital safety information easier to access and understand.

To provide users with a simple platform for reporting suspicious digital activity while improving awareness of common online scam and phishing warning signs.

VISION

Safer and more informed digital communities.

To contribute towards digital spaces where users are better equipped to recognise suspicious activity, make informed decisions and practise safer online behaviour.

PROJECT SCOPE

Built as a learning prototype.

Ngao is currently an academic prototype. Reports and classifications in this version use dummy or locally stored data and should not be treated as official cybersecurity assessments.
